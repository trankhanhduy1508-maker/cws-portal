# Spec 007 — Founder Idea Gates

## Goal
Preserve Founder future business/product ideas without allowing them to distract the current CWS MVP or be implemented before explicit evidence-backed trigger conditions are met.

## Current idea
**Code Factory / Upwork software-building service**: reuse lessons, governance, AI-assisted engineering workflows and reusable patterns learned from CWS to build software/web products for external clients.

## Activation gate
This initiative remains **DORMANT** until CWS has evidence that it can serve approximately **100 real concurrent customers/users in a production-like or production workload**, not merely a local simulation or unit/load-test harness.

## Rules
- Before the gate: record/preserve ideas only; no implementation, service launch, Upwork offering, dedicated infrastructure, or diversion of the current CWS bottleneck.
- Trigger must be grounded in runtime evidence; simulated 100-user tests alone do not unlock it.
- When the gate is reached, AI must report to Founder that the initiative is eligible for activation and request an explicit Founder decision before implementation.
- Future ideas may be added to the same Founder idea registry with their own activation gates.
