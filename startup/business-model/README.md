Current direction captured from Founder discussions:
- prefer genuine word-of-mouth over paid ads in the early stage;
- first prove real customers pay, receive value and return;
- defer mission/task rewards because they may create spam without verified revenue;
- defer a broad Free Compute Pool until CWS has an economic source to fund it;
- investigate Render Credits as a product-native reward;
- later referral/affiliate design may offer a lower cash reward or a higher-value Render Credit reward;
- reward should follow verified value/revenue rather than clicks, shares or signups;
- all reward percentages discussed so far remain hypotheses until unit economics are proven.

Reasoning loop:
CUSTOMER PAIN -> VALUE CREATED -> WHO PAYS -> UNIT ECONOMICS -> DISTRIBUTION -> RETENTION -> SANDBOX -> MEASURE -> DECIDE.
