# CWS Business Experiment Backlog

Status: future sandbox candidates only.

## Priority candidate after reliable paid jobs exist
### Referral Credits
Hypothesis: satisfied users can bring new paying customers more efficiently than paid advertising.
Success evidence: referred first paid jobs, repeat usage, acceptable acquisition cost and positive contribution economics.

## Deferred
### Mission rewards
Deferred because actions such as sharing can create spam and may not produce verified revenue.

### Free Compute Pool
Deferred until CWS has an economic funding source and real paid-job economics.

### Loyalty Credits
Test only after repeat paid usage exists. Measure whether credits increase retention without consuming excessive margin.

### Affiliate cash vs credits
Later professional partners may be offered a lower cash commission or higher-value Render Credits. Percentages must be derived from real contribution margin, not guessed.

## Experiment rule
Every business experiment should define hypothesis, baseline, success metric, failure/kill condition, economic budget, anti-abuse boundary and promotion gate before launch.
