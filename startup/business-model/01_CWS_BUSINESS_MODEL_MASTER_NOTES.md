# CWS Business Model Master Notes

Status: research notes.

CWS should focus on customer outcome: remote rendering frees the customer's workstation and reduces deadline pressure while using partner-PC capacity.

CWS economically connects render demand with partner-PC supply and adds scheduling, security, storage, rendering, payment and trust.

Use contribution-margin thinking rather than treating customer price minus host payout as net profit.

Before scaling growth, prove real jobs, willingness to pay, host economics, positive contribution potential, repeat usage and genuine recommendation.

Current Founder preference is to learn from real users and word-of-mouth before relying on paid advertising.

Potential later layers include transaction margin, paid urgency/certainty, recurring studio usage, Render Credits and professional affiliates.

Rule: sandbox business-model changes and measure real conversion, retention and economics before promotion.
