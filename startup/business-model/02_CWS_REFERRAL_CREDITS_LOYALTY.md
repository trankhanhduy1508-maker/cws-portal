# CWS Referral, Credits and Loyalty Notes

Status: research only.

Current hypotheses:
- referral should reward verified value, not shares/clicks;
- mission/task rewards are deferred because spam risk is high and revenue impact is uncertain;
- Render Credits may be more useful than generic points because they map directly to future CWS usage;
- a future referrer may be offered a lower cash reward or a higher Render Credit reward;
- cash and credit percentages must be bounded by real contribution margin;
- Credits should not automatically be treated as withdrawable cash;
- professional affiliate programs should be separated from normal customer referral.

Candidate future flow:
existing satisfied customer -> referred customer -> first real settled paid job -> verified reward eligibility -> cash or higher-value Render Credits.

No percentages in this file are approved pricing policy.
